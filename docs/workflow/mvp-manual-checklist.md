# MVP Manual Validation Checklist

- **Today generation**: Launch app, verify Today shows 6–8 blocks with Warm-Up first and a fun ending last; block durations look reasonable.
- **Start session**: Start from Today, see the preview countdown, tap to begin; confirm concentric rings and block title render.
- **In-session controls**: Pause/resume works; swipe left on the horizontal timer area to decrease time, swipe right to add time; skip advances to next block. Metronome: tap icon to toggle, swipe left/right in the horizontal metronome area to adjust BPM (no +/- buttons), BPM updates visibly.
- **Instructions & references**: Tap the instructions list/card to bring up details (and reference diagram if available); when a questionmark icon appears, open the reference sheet and see the diagram without crashes.
- **Feedback & progression**: Finish a block, submit feedback (Easy/Good/Hard), and proceed to the next block until the session ends.
- **Summary & history**: Summary shows block feedback + minutes; closing it returns to Today/History; History increments by one session with correct titles and feedback.
